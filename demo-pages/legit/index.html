How to pay a traffic fine in the UAE safely

Guides · 24 November 2026 · 4 min read

Fake "90% discount" offers on traffic fines keep circulating on social media and in text messages. Police in Abu Dhabi and Dubai have said these offers are not real. Here is how to check and pay a fine through official channels only.

Where to pay

  • Abu Dhabi: the TAMM app or tamm.abudhabi, or the Abu Dhabi Police app.
  • Dubai: the RTA app or rta.ae, or the Dubai Police app.
  • Other emirates: the MOI UAE app or moi.gov.ae.
Rule of thumb: type the address yourself or use the official app. Don't pay through a link in a text, a WhatsApp message or a social media post.

Signs of a fake fine page

  • A huge discount and a countdown timer.
  • It asks for your OTP or card PIN. No official site does.
  • The web address is not the official one, even if the logo looks right.

Suspect a scam? In Abu Dhabi, report it to the Aman service on 800 2626. In Dubai, use the eCrime platform or call 901.
